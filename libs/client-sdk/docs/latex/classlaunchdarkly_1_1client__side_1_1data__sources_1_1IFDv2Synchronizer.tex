\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
A continuous data source that produces a stream of results, used to keep flag data current once initialization is complete.

The underlying connection is started lazily on the first call to \mbox{\hyperlink{classlaunchdarkly_1_1client__side_1_1data__sources_1_1IFDv2Synchronizer_a371aa6161cecbd98d9006dc10194ae91}{Next()}} and runs until \mbox{\hyperlink{classlaunchdarkly_1_1client__side_1_1data__sources_1_1IFDv2Synchronizer_ad39d53759a04d65036d1f97fe4202280}{Close()}} is called.

Implementations must be thread-\/safe to the extent this contract needs\+: \mbox{\hyperlink{classlaunchdarkly_1_1client__side_1_1data__sources_1_1IFDv2Synchronizer_a371aa6161cecbd98d9006dc10194ae91}{Next()}} is called from one thread at a time, and \mbox{\hyperlink{classlaunchdarkly_1_1client__side_1_1data__sources_1_1IFDv2Synchronizer_ad39d53759a04d65036d1f97fe4202280}{Close()}} may be called concurrently with it from another.

Returns a Future that resolves with the next result once it is available.

On the first call, the synchronizer starts its underlying connection. Subsequent calls continue reading from the same connection.

\mbox{\hyperlink{classlaunchdarkly_1_1client__side_1_1data__sources_1_1IFDv2Synchronizer_ad39d53759a04d65036d1f97fe4202280}{Close()}} may be called from another thread to unblock \mbox{\hyperlink{classlaunchdarkly_1_1client__side_1_1data__sources_1_1IFDv2Synchronizer_a371aa6161cecbd98d9006dc10194ae91}{Next()}}, in which case the future resolves with \mbox{\hyperlink{structlaunchdarkly_1_1client__side_1_1data__sources_1_1FDv2SourceResult_1_1Shutdown}{FDv2\+Source\+Result\+::\+Shutdown}}.
