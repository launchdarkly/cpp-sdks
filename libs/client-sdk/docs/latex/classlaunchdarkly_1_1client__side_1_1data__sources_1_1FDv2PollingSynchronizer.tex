\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
Keeps flag data current by polling the FDv2 client polling endpoint on an interval.

Polls are rate limited. A poll that would come sooner than the interval allows is delayed by the time remaining, so that repeated activations of this synchronizer cannot produce a burst of requests.

Threading model\+: \mbox{\hyperlink{classlaunchdarkly_1_1client__side_1_1data__sources_1_1FDv2PollingSynchronizer_acd20a573ec8c3741164805d82c6c2324}{Next()}} should only be called once at a time. \mbox{\hyperlink{classlaunchdarkly_1_1client__side_1_1data__sources_1_1FDv2PollingSynchronizer_ad0fa35d5a616f972b92660c088e24a88}{Close()}} may be called concurrently with \mbox{\hyperlink{classlaunchdarkly_1_1client__side_1_1data__sources_1_1FDv2PollingSynchronizer_acd20a573ec8c3741164805d82c6c2324}{Next()}}. This object may be safely destroyed once no call to \mbox{\hyperlink{classlaunchdarkly_1_1client__side_1_1data__sources_1_1FDv2PollingSynchronizer_acd20a573ec8c3741164805d82c6c2324}{Next()}} or \mbox{\hyperlink{classlaunchdarkly_1_1client__side_1_1data__sources_1_1FDv2PollingSynchronizer_ad0fa35d5a616f972b92660c088e24a88}{Close()}} is in progress.
