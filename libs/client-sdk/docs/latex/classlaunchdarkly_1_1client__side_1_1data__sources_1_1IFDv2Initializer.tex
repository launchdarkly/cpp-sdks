\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
A one-\/shot data source that runs to completion and returns a single result, used to load a basis before handing off to an \mbox{\hyperlink{classlaunchdarkly_1_1client__side_1_1data__sources_1_1IFDv2Synchronizer}{IFDv2\+Synchronizer}}.

Implementations must be thread-\/safe to the extent this contract needs\+: \mbox{\hyperlink{classlaunchdarkly_1_1client__side_1_1data__sources_1_1IFDv2Initializer_aa2ce3ebca088ab633002d41ffde0daa2}{Run()}} is called at most once, and \mbox{\hyperlink{classlaunchdarkly_1_1client__side_1_1data__sources_1_1IFDv2Initializer_a3ba4036d82984698fb82fce698306543}{Close()}} may be called concurrently with it from another thread.
